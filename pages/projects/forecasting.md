A reproducible data science pipeline over **12.6M+ transactions** from about **2,500 stores**, covering data cleaning, feature engineering, demand forecasting, and inventory optimization.

Compared classical time-series methods (ARIMA, SARIMA, ETS) against gradient-boosted models (LightGBM, XGBoost), using chronological validation and held-out testing.
